## Purpose

An idea becoming a durable, timezone-correct, policy-carrying row — captured on
a phone, tolerant of no network, and idempotent when the queue drains.

## Requirements


### Requirement: Capture records the client's instant, not the server's

An entry SHALL record the moment of capture as supplied by the client, together
with the device's IANA timezone and its UTC offset at that instant. The server
MUST NOT substitute its own clock.

The identifier SHALL be generated by the client, and the timestamp embedded in
it MUST agree with the recorded capture instant.

#### Scenario: Immediate capture
- **WHEN** an entry is captured while online and submitted at once
- **THEN** the stored capture instant is the client's, and the stored timezone and offset are the device's at that moment

#### Scenario: Queue drained hours later
- **WHEN** an entry captured at 23:10 is submitted at 07:30 the next morning
- **THEN** the stored capture instant is 23:10, not the time of receipt

#### Scenario: Identifier and timestamp agree
- **WHEN** an entry is stored
- **THEN** the timestamp embedded in its identifier equals its recorded capture instant, so ordering by time and by identifier cannot disagree

#### Scenario: Offset resolves an ambiguous local time
- **WHEN** an entry is captured during a repeated wall-clock hour at a daylight-saving transition
- **THEN** the recorded offset distinguishes which of the two instants occurred, without needing to re-derive it later

### Requirement: Client and server instants are both recorded

The system SHALL record the server's receipt instant alongside the client's
capture instant. The client's instant is authoritative and MUST NOT be rejected,
clamped or corrected, however implausible.

A disagreement between the two is preserved rather than resolved, so a device
with a wrong clock is diagnosable after the fact instead of silently corrupting
the record.

#### Scenario: Implausible future timestamp
- **WHEN** an entry arrives whose capture instant is ahead of server time
- **THEN** it is stored unchanged, the receipt instant is recorded beside it, and the entry is not rejected

#### Scenario: Legitimately old timestamp
- **WHEN** a three-day-old queued entry drains
- **THEN** its capture instant is preserved exactly and is not treated as an error

#### Scenario: Skew is diagnosable
- **WHEN** a device's clock is wrong by a known amount
- **THEN** the difference between the two recorded instants makes that visible without inspecting anything outside the row

### Requirement: Entries awaiting sync are visible as a count only

The capture surface SHALL show a persistent count of entries captured locally
and not yet acknowledged, so that a user has no reason to re-submit.

It MUST NOT render pending entries individually. There is deliberately no
per-item surface, because a per-item surface is what a retry, edit or delete
control would attach to — and per `NOT_BUILDING.md` that is task management.

#### Scenario: Entries waiting to sync
- **WHEN** two entries are captured with no connectivity
- **THEN** the surface shows that two are waiting, without listing them

#### Scenario: Count clears on drain
- **WHEN** connectivity returns and the queue drains
- **THEN** the count returns to zero without user action

#### Scenario: No per-item controls exist
- **WHEN** the pending indicator is displayed
- **THEN** it offers no action against any individual entry

### Requirement: Capture succeeds without a network

The capture surface SHALL accept and durably queue an entry with no network
available, and submit it when connectivity returns. Queueing MUST complete
locally before any network attempt.

#### Scenario: Captured offline
- **WHEN** an idea is captured with no connectivity
- **THEN** it is stored locally with its identifier, capture instant, timezone and policy already fixed, and the user is told it is captured

#### Scenario: Drained on reconnect
- **WHEN** connectivity returns with entries queued
- **THEN** each is submitted unchanged, and nothing about the entry is decided at submission time

### Requirement: Replay is idempotent and silent

Submitting an entry the server already holds SHALL succeed and return the stored
entry. It MUST NOT create a second row, MUST NOT return an error, and MUST NOT
record a failure.

Where a replayed identifier arrives with different content, the stored entry
SHALL be kept unchanged and the divergence recorded.

#### Scenario: Duplicate submission
- **WHEN** the same entry is submitted twice
- **THEN** one row exists, both requests succeed, and the second returns the stored entry

#### Scenario: Duplicate does not pollute the failure ledger
- **WHEN** a duplicate is submitted
- **THEN** no failure row is written, so routine client retries cannot dominate failure recurrence counts

#### Scenario: Replayed identifier with altered content
- **WHEN** an identifier already stored is submitted with different text
- **THEN** the stored entry is unchanged and the divergence is recorded as an event against that entry

### Requirement: Policy is recorded as intent

An entry SHALL record the policy the user chose, unmodified. The system MUST NOT
substitute, downgrade or reject a policy because the current profile cannot
honor it — a policy the profile cannot honor is a quarantine decision made later, not a
capture-time rewrite.

#### Scenario: Local-only captured on a profile that cannot honor it
- **WHEN** a `local-only` entry is captured while the resolved profile has no local reasoner
- **THEN** the entry is stored with `local-only` intact and is quarantined rather than rewritten or refused

### Requirement: The capture surface is served a capability report

The capture surface SHALL obtain available policies as a report in which
unavailable policies are present and marked with the finding that caused their
unavailability. The surface MUST NOT be served a filtered list of selectable
policies.

#### Scenario: Degraded profile
- **WHEN** the capture surface requests policies on a profile that cannot honor `local-only`
- **THEN** `local-only` is returned marked unavailable, with the specific probe finding as its reason, and is rendered disabled rather than hidden

### Requirement: Entries are eligible for a run only when they are complete

An entry SHALL be eligible for dispatch only when it is queued, is not synthetic,
and carries content to reason about. An entry that can never satisfy this MUST
NOT be silently invisible; it is retrievable with the reason it is ineligible.

#### Scenario: Synthetic entries are never dispatched
- **WHEN** the synthetic night generator writes queued entries
- **THEN** the orchestrator's eligible set excludes them

#### Scenario: An entry with no content
- **WHEN** a text entry exists whose body is empty
- **THEN** it is not eligible for dispatch, and it is retrievable as ineligible rather than absent

#### Scenario: A captured entry is reachable by the night
- **WHEN** an entry is captured normally
- **THEN** it appears in the eligible set without any further action

### Requirement: Capture derives what it can without a model

A title SHALL be derived from the entry's own content by deterministic means.
Capture MUST NOT invoke a reasoning model, because that would make every capture
depend on a priced cloud model and a policy-carrying invocation.

Location SHALL come from configuration, with an optional per-entry override.
Capture MUST NOT request device geolocation.

#### Scenario: Title derived
- **WHEN** an entry is captured with no title
- **THEN** a title is derived from its content and no model call is recorded

#### Scenario: Location from configuration
- **WHEN** an entry is captured without an explicit location
- **THEN** the configured home coordinates are recorded and no geolocation permission is requested

### Requirement: Deployment identity is server-derived

Whether an entry is synthetic SHALL be determined by the server from its
deployment, never accepted from the request. The capture path MUST be identical
in the personal and judge deployments.

#### Scenario: Client cannot mark an entry synthetic
- **WHEN** a request asserts that its entry is synthetic
- **THEN** the assertion is ignored and the server's own determination is recorded

#### Scenario: Same path in both deployments
- **WHEN** the judge deployment captures an entry
- **THEN** it uses the same handler, with no deployment-specific branch
